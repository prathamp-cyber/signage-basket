Shipping & Installation

How your sign reaches you — whether by our installation crew or by courier across India.

On-site installation

Our installation crew covers Gandhidham, Adipur, Anjar, Bhuj, Mundra, Mandvi, Bhachau, and other areas across Kutch and Gujarat. Installation is carried out by appointment at a date and time that suits you.

Installation charges are quoted separately and depend on the location, type of sign, and wall or surface conditions. We will confirm the installation charge as part of your overall quote before you approve the order.

Delivery across India

For clients outside our installation service area, we dispatch signs via reputable courier partners using appropriate protective packaging. This includes:

  • Foam padding and bubble wrap for fragile acrylic and nameplate products
  • Rigid crating or double-boxing for LED sign boards and large 3D letter sets
  • Mounting hardware and installation instructions included in the package

We will share a tracking number once the package has been dispatched.

Typical lead times

Lead times begin from the date you approve the design preview. Typical times are:

  • Home nameplates and small acrylic boards: approximately 5 to 7 working days
  • LED glow signs and 3D letter sets: approximately 7 to 10 working days
  • Large commercial or complex installations: timeline confirmed individually at the quote stage

These are estimates and may vary. We will keep you informed if there is an expected delay.

Transit damage

If your sign arrives damaged in transit, please:

  • Photograph the damaged packaging and the product before unpacking fully
  • Report the damage to us by WhatsApp or email within 48 hours of receipt

Reports made after 48 hours may not be eligible for a claim. We will work with you to resolve transit damage cases as quickly as possible.
