Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

Signage Basket ("we", "our", or "us") is a custom signage and nameplate studio based in Gandhidham, Kutch, Gujarat, India. This policy explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it, and how we handle it when you use our website or contact us for a quote.

What information we collect

We may collect the following when you submit a quote request through our website form or contact us on WhatsApp:

  • Your name
  • Your phone number or WhatsApp number
  • Your email address (optional)
  • Your city or location
  • Details about the sign or nameplate you want
  • Any images or files you send us directly

We do not collect payment card details, passwords, or government identity numbers.

How we use your information

We use the information you provide solely to:

  • Respond to your quote request
  • Prepare and share a free 3D design preview
  • Communicate about your order or project
  • Arrange delivery or installation

We do not use your information for unsolicited marketing or sell it to any third party.

Third-party services

Our website and business use the following third-party services. Their own privacy policies apply when you interact with them:

  • WhatsApp — When you click a WhatsApp link on our website, you are taken to the WhatsApp platform, which is governed by Meta's privacy policy.
  • Google Maps — Our contact page embeds a Google Maps frame to show our location. Google's privacy policy governs that interaction.
  • Google Fonts — Typography on this website is loaded from Google Fonts servers. Google's privacy policy applies.

Data storage and retention

Quote request details you submit through our form are sent directly to us via WhatsApp. We do not store your data in a database or on our web server. We retain the information in our WhatsApp conversation for as long as it is needed to complete your project, then we delete it.

Cookies

This website does not set its own tracking cookies. Third-party scripts (such as Google Fonts or embedded maps) may set cookies according to their own policies.

Your rights

You can request that we delete any personal information we hold about you by emailing us at hello@signagebasket.com. We will comply within a reasonable time.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy occasionally. The date at the top of this page will reflect the most recent revision.

Contact

If you have any questions about this policy, please contact us at hello@signagebasket.com or through WhatsApp at the number listed on our contact page.
